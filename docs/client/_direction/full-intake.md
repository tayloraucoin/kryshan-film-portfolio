About Me page:

Born and raised in BC, I’ve been making films for as long as I can remember.

My career started with producing two fast film contests, The 24 Hour Film Contest and The Great Canadian Commercial Contest.

Through these events, I met many of my favourite collaborators and started making short films on weekends with them.

I directed two horror shorts for the Bloodshots Film Festival, Jack and The Bully Solution. They won the Audience Choice Award and Grand Jury Prize, and screened at many of the world's most acclaimed genre festivals including Sitges, Fantasia and Fantastic Fest.

With the support of a Directors Guild of Canada / BC film grant Kickstart, I co-wrote and directed Glimpse which premiered at Vancouver International Film Festival and won the National Screen Institute’s A&E Short Filmmakers Award. I sold Glimpse to Corus Entertainment, who screened it on the Sundance Channel and Movieola.

These accolades led to me getting work as a director for hire, including PSAs for the Directors Guild of Canada, Creative BC, MPIAA and IATSE 669; social media ads for the Shotlister app; TV pitch sizzle reels Promoter and The Shameless Traveller; four episodes of the web series Libelle later incorporated into the feature film Mission Ninety Two: Dragonfly; and eight opening films for Crazy8’s. In recent years I've directed Ted Danson, Kevin Smith, Aubrey Plaza and Mary Steenbergen for various social media spots and PSAs.

I’ve also shot and edited hundreds of news segments, corporate and behind the scenes work, and other non-fiction projects, often as a one-man crew. Clients have included Disney, Netflix, Legendary Pictures, and former Mayor Gregor Robertson. I’m a member of the IATSE 669 camera union in the EPK category.

Most recently as an editor, I was nominated for co-editing the short dark comedy A Night at Malibuz.

As a film instructor I teach directing, shooting and editing at Vancouver Film School and LaSalle College, and do individual coaching and mentorship. I've also run filmmaking programs and camps in Mexico, Victoria, Toronto and the Northwest Territories; founded Frames Film Project for at-risk youth, in collaboration with Frog Hollow Neighborhood House; and taught at InFocus Film School for twelve years.

Outside of the industry, I practice transcendental meditation, ski and play beach volleyball, DJ, and host large-scale immersive  events.

Born and raised in BC, I’ve been making films for as long as I can remember.

My career started with producing two fast film contests, The 24 Hour Film Contest and The Great Canadian Commercial Contest.

Through these events, I met many of my favourite collaborators and started making short films on weekends with them.

### Jack (2009)
- Their role: Director
- Kind: Short Film
- For: Passion project
- Story: Jack was written, shot and edited in 48 hours for the Bloodshots Film Festival. Genre legend Dan O'Bannon (creator/writer of Alien and writer of Total Recall) judged the film a few weeks before his passing, praising the effective mix of comedy and horror.
- Awards or selections: Grand Jury Prize, the Audience Choice Award, Best Script and Best Death, Bloodshots Film Festival

Jack subsequently screened at the CFC Worldwide Short Film Festival where it was praised by numerous press outlets including Exclaim! Jack was also included on a list of The Shorts Report's most watchable Canadian short films of the year.

Jack was distributed for seven years by Shorts International, the primary distributor of short films for iTunes.
